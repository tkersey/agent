# Checked compilation in Agent

Boundary compilation and final closed linking always invoke checked P01.
Agent admission precedes Boundary compilation. Typed/direct and compiled-tool
paths forward the selected shared compilation contract and its limits; open BMO1
components defer whole-program optimization until final linking. No off/safe
production selector remains.

`agent.compileObserved` accepts Boundary options through `boundary_options`.
Choose the semantic contract only where the consumer's required observations
allow it. Classification, approvals, quotas, exact proposal binding, cleanup and
cancellation remain application obligations. Codecs and existing World images
are not silently optimized.

See [Agent qualification](../conformance/agent4/evidence.md) and the
[coordinated acceptance report](https://github.com/tkersey/boundary/blob/codex/canonical-durable-3183/docs/optimization-acceptance.md). The actual source/package/runtime
binding remains in dependencies.lock.json; historical performance reports keep
their exact original identities in the immutable archive index.
